Disabled all 11 optional apps outside the selected priority list, in four small batches with an inspected TV screenshot and actual disabled-package list between each. No app data erased and nothing uninstalled.

hoichoi, Rakuten Viki, MX Player TV, JioCinema, Google Play Movies & TV, Google Play Games, Gallery, USB Media Player, T-Solo, MagiConnect and TCL Channel are disabled. The 13 kept streaming/music apps, Google Play Store/Services, voice assistant, Chromecast, Miracast and firmware updates remain enabled. The updated priority theme is being applied as the next step.
