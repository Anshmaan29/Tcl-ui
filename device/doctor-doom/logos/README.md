# Favourites logos

Small transparent 320×180 PNG canvases, decoded only for the favourites row. Five logos total about 28 KB of storage and about 1.1 MB of decoded RGBA pixels. They are outside `wallpapers/` so they never enter background rotation.

- Netflix and YouTube: white paths from [Simple Icons](https://simpleicons.org/), rendered locally.
- Prime: the smile/arrow from [Simple Icons v11 Amazon artwork](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/blob/11.0.0/icons/amazon.svg), rendered white and cropped locally.
- JioHotstar: the white star isolated from the owner's supplied reference image.
- Apple TV: the owner's supplied pearlescent artwork, with its black background made transparent locally while preserving its colours.

The filenames are the installed Android package names. To replace a logo, keep its filename and push the theme. No PlayStation logo or tile was added. These brand marks remain their respective owners' marks.
